💜 About the Author

Hello! My name is Malinda Dollack.

I am a retired piano teacher and registered nurse. I am married and have been fortunate to share 37 wonderful years with my husband.

Most importantly, I am a born-again, Spirit-filled follower of Jesus Christ. For many years, I believed my husband was the evangelist while I supported him through prayer at home. Recently, God reminded me that I am also an evangelist—as are all His followers. We simply have different audiences.

My world has always involved children. Although my husband and I could not have children together, and my daughter passed away at 2 1/2 years old from a relationship i had before I met my husband, I have always believed we can still be an important part of children’s lives even when they are not biologically our own.

God has blessed us with a daughter from my husband’s previous relationship, as well as grandchildren and great-grandchildren. As a piano teacher, I taught children as young as three. God did not give me more biological children, but He gave me many other children to love, teach, and share the Gospel of Jesus Christ with.

Thank you for your interest in these wonderful books that the Holy Spirit inspired me to write.

With love in Christ,Malinda Dollack
