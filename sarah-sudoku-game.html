Sarah the Baby Sheep: My Shepherd, Jesus's Birth. The Christmas Story Sudoku Game

How to Play: Fill every empty square so that each row, each column, and each 3x3 box contains each picture 1 through 9 one time.

Click a picture tile in the column, then click an empty square. You can also drag a tile into an empty square. Click a filled square to remove your answer.

When you win Sarah the Sheep will appear with a Very Important Message JESUS LOVES YOU !
